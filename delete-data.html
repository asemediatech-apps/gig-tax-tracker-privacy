Delete Your Data — Gig Tax Tracker

Gig Tax Tracker stores your tax, income, mileage, and expense data only on your device. We do not operate a server that holds your data, so there is no account data for us to delete. You can erase all of your data yourself, at any time.

How to delete your data in the app

  1. Open Gig Tax Tracker.
  2. Go to Settings → Clear All Data.
  3. Confirm. All income, expense, and mileage records and settings are permanently deleted from your device.

Uninstalling the app also deletes the local database. Note: local backup CSV files in external storage may remain until you delete them yourself (they are in your device's files under the app's folder).

Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Ads in the app are served by Google AdMob. Advertising-related data (such as your Advertising ID) is handled by Google, not by us. You can manage it in your device Settings → Google → Ads ("Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"), or via Google My Ad Center.

Request help

If you need help deleting your data, email us at asemediatech@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data — Gig Tax Tracker". We will reply within 7 days. Because we do not hold your data on any server, the steps above are the way your data is deleted.
