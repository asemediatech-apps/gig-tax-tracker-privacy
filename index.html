Privacy Policy — Gig Tax Tracker

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Gig Tax Tracker ("the App") is developed by ASEMedia Tech ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your choices. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Data stored on your device

The App is offline-first. The tax and mileage data you enter — income entries, expense entries, mileage logs, and your settings — is stored only on your device in a local database. We do not operate a server that receives this data, and we cannot see it.

Optional local backups (automatic weekly backup and manual "Backup now") write CSV files to your device's external files directory. Those files stay on your device unless you choose to share or export them.

If you use the CSV export feature, the file is created on your device for you to share with your accountant or tax software. We do not receive a copy.

2. Location data (GPS mileage tracking)

If you enable GPS auto-tracking, the App uses your device's location in the foreground while tracking a trip, to measure distance driven. The App records only the trip distance, date, time range, and purpose. It does not record or store your route, coordinates, or specific locations. Location data never leaves your device. GPS tracking is off by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the following data to serve and measure ads:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address) for ad targeting
  • App interaction data (ad impressions and clicks)

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device Settings → Google → Ads ("Opt out of Ads Personalization") or by resetting your Advertising ID. The App does not share your tax, income, mileage, or expense data with AdMob or any advertiser.

4. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or share your name, email address, phone number, financial account details, or government identifiers. The App requires no account and no sign-in.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. The only third party that receives data from the App is Google AdMob, as described in Section 3. Your tax, income, mileage, and expense data is never shared with any third party by the App.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device for as long as you keep the App installed. You can delete all of your data at any time from Settings → Clear All Data. Uninstalling the App deletes the local database (note: local backup CSV files in external storage may remain until you delete them).

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults (18+) who work as independent contractors or self-employed workers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Security

Because your tax data never leaves your device, there is no server-side breach risk for that data. AdMob data handling is governed by Google's security practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at asemediatech@gmail.com.
